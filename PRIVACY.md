# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: July 2026_

## Introduction

SondeAlert respects your privacy.

This page provides a summary of how the application processes information. A complete privacy policy will be published before the official Google Play release.

---

## Location

SondeAlert uses your device's location only to:

- Display your current position on the map.
- Calculate the distance to nearby radiosondes.
- Provide optional distance alerts.

Your location is processed locally on your device.

---

## Downloaded Data

Downloaded radiosonde information is stored locally on your device.

This data is used only by SondeAlert to display nearby radiosondes and improve performance.

---

## Personal Information

SondeAlert does not require an account.

The application does not intentionally collect personal information.

---

## Data Sharing

SondeAlert does not sell or share your personal information.

---

## Third-Party Services

SondeAlert makes use of:

- Radiosondy.info (radiosonde data)
- MapLibre (map rendering)
- OpenStreetMap contributors (map data)

These services operate under their own terms and privacy policies.

---

## Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated as SondeAlert evolves.

Any significant changes will be announced through GitHub and future application releases.

---

Thank you for trusting SondeAlert.
